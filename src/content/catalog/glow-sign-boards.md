If your business operates after dark, a highly visible, illuminated sign is your most powerful marketing tool. At Classic Printing, we specialize in manufacturing premium **LED Glow Sign Boards** that capture attention and drive footfall.

## The Power of LED Technology

Historically, glow signs relied on fragile neon tubes or standard fluorescent tube lights, which suffered from flickering, high power consumption, and frequent blowouts. 

We manufacture our glow signs using **100% advanced LED technology**:
- **Energy Efficiency:** Our LED modules consume up to 80% less electricity than traditional tubes, drastically reducing your monthly overhead.
- **Uniform Lighting:** We use precision-spaced LED injection modules with optical lenses. This ensures the face of your sign glows evenly, without any ugly dark spots or visible light bulbs (hot spots).
- **IP67 Waterproofing:** Our outdoor glow signs are built with waterproof LED modules and heavy-duty SMPS power supplies, ensuring they survive heavy monsoons and foggy winters.

## Types of Glow Signs

### Standard Backlit Flex Boards
The most cost-effective option for large formats. We use a heavy-duty metal box frame, pack it with LEDs, and stretch a translucent backlit flex over the front. Perfect for large retail showrooms in Zirakpur.

### Backlit Acrylic Box Signs
For a more premium, glossy finish, we use cast acrylic sheets instead of flex. We then apply UV printing or translucent cut-vinyl to the face. These are highly popular for clinics, cafes, and boutiques in Chandigarh and Mohali.
