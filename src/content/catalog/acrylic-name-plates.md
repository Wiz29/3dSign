**Acrylic Name Plates** offer the premium, glossy aesthetic of heavy glass, but without the extreme fragility or weight. They are highly versatile and are the most popular choice for both residential homes and corporate office doors across the Tricity.

## Infinite Customization

Because acrylic is a synthetic polymer, it allows for incredible design flexibility:
- **Clear Acrylic (Glass Look):** A highly polished clear sheet mounted with silver standoff studs. Your name is either UV printed on the back or applied via vinyl on the front, creating a beautiful drop-shadow on the wall behind it.
- **Frosted Acrylic:** Gives the look of sand-blasted or etched glass, offering a highly sophisticated, glare-free finish.
- **Opaque Colored Acrylic:** Glossy black or white acrylic bases layered with contrasting 3D acrylic letters.

## Perfect for Offices

In addition to homes, acrylic is the standard for corporate environments. We manufacture desk name plates, cabin door signs, and directional plates for hospitals and IT offices in Mohali.
