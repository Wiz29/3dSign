Before you can stretch a massive flex banner in the sky, you need an absolutely solid foundation. Classic Printing provides expert **Structural Fabrication** for permanent hoardings and commercial billboards.

## Engineering & Safety First

Building a hoarding structure is a serious engineering task. A 40ft x 20ft hoarding acts as a massive sail catching the wind. Without proper structural integrity, it poses a severe safety hazard during the monsoon storms in Punjab.

At Classic Printing, safety is our primary concern:
- **Heavy-Duty Materials:** We fabricate our structures using thick Mild Steel (MS) angles, channels, and pipes. 
- **Deep Foundations:** For ground-mounted structures, we pour deep RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) foundations to anchor the massive steel columns.
- **Wind-Load Engineering:** Our structures are cross-braced heavily at the back to withstand extreme wind pressure.

## Custom Fabrication

We fabricate hoarding structures tailored to your specific location:
1. **Rooftop Hoardings:** Securely anchored to the RCC beams of commercial buildings in Zirakpur and Mohali.
2. **Ground-Mounted Hoardings:** Massive structures erected on empty plots or along highways.
3. **V-Shape Hoardings:** Dual-faced structures designed for visibility from both directions of traffic.

We also paint all our MS structures with premium anti-rust primers and weather-resistant enamel paint to ensure decades of structural integrity. Trust Classic Printing for safe, legally compliant, and rock-solid hoarding structures.
