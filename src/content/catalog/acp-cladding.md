Does the exterior of your commercial building look old, uneven, or outdated? **ACP Cladding** is the fastest and most effective way to completely modernize your building's facade.

## What is ACP Cladding?

ACP (Aluminum Composite Panel) cladding involves covering the exterior walls of a building with large, perfectly flat, metallic panels. We create a grid framework of lightweight aluminum or MS tubes on your building's exterior wall, and then securely rivet or screw the ACP sheets to this framework.

The result is a flawless, ultra-modern exterior that looks like a brand-new high-tech building.

## Why Clad Your Building?

ACP cladding is standard for modern corporate offices, showrooms, and hospitals across Chandigarh and Mohali for several reasons:

- **Instant Modernization:** It hides old brickwork, cracked plaster, and messy wiring, providing a sleek, highly professional look.
- **Weather Protection:** ACP is completely waterproof and protects your actual building walls from harsh sun and heavy monsoons. 
- **Thermal Insulation:** The gap between the ACP and the wall acts as insulation, helping keep your building cooler in the Punjab summers.
- **Low Maintenance:** Unlike paint which peels and fades, ACP retains its color for years and can be easily washed clean.

## The Perfect Base for Signage

ACP cladding provides the ultimate flat surface for mounting massive 3D Glow Signs or Acrylic Logos. We can run all the electrical wiring invisibly behind the ACP panels, resulting in a totally clean, wire-free storefront.
