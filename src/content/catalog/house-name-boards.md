For large independent villas, estates, and farmhouses in areas like Kharar, Zirakpur, and New Chandigarh, a small standard name plate often gets lost on a massive exterior boundary wall. What you need is a large format **House Name Board**.

## Grand Entrances Require Grand Signage

A house name board is significantly larger than a standard plate. It is designed to be highly visible from the road and to complement massive entry gates and high boundary walls.

### Materials Used:
- **Thick ACP Bases:** We often use large ACP (Aluminum Composite Panel) sheets with wooden or metallic textures as the base for the board, as they can withstand extreme weather without warping.
- **Large 3D Letters:** We mount large acrylic or steel 3D letters onto the board. 
- **Integrated Lighting:** We embed LED modules inside the letters or use hidden LED strip lighting to ensure the massive board glows beautifully at night.
