When you need towering visibility that commands the skyline, an **Outdoor Unipole** is the premium choice for outdoor advertising. 

## What is a Unipole?

A unipole (or monopole) is a massive advertising billboard mounted atop a single, thick, heavy-duty steel column. Because they are elevated high above the ground, they offer unobstructed visibility from long distances, bypassing trees, buildings, and ground-level traffic.

They are the preferred advertising medium along major expressways, such as the Zirakpur-Patiala highway and the Mohali Airport Road.

## Heavy-Duty Fabrication

Fabricating and erecting a unipole requires specialized industrial expertise. Classic Printing handles the entire turnkey project:

1. **Massive Foundations:** We excavate deep foundation pits and pour highly reinforced concrete to anchor the base plate.
2. **Thick MS Pipes:** We use industrial-grade, thick-walled Mild Steel pipes for the main column to ensure it can bear the immense weight and wind-load of the top billboard.
3. **Catwalks & Illumination:** We fabricate safe catwalks (maintenance platforms) around the back of the unipole. We also install heavy-duty metal halide or LED floodlights to ensure your advertisement is visible all night.

## Types of Unipoles

- **Standard Front-Facing:** A single large board facing oncoming traffic.
- **V-Shape Unipoles:** Two boards angled outward, capturing traffic from both directions simultaneously.
- **Tri-Vision Unipoles:** Three boards forming a triangle for 360-degree visibility at major roundabouts.
