If you want your brand to literally stand out, 3D letters are the ultimate signage solution. By adding depth and texture to your typography, 3D signs create a powerful, permanent, and highly professional image for your business.

## Materials We Use

At Classic Printing, we fabricate 3D letters using state-of-the-art CNC routers and laser cutters, ensuring absolute precision for your brand's unique font and logo. 

### 1. Cast Acrylic 3D Letters
The most popular choice for illuminated signs. Acrylic letters can be front-lit (the face of the letter glows) or edge-lit. They are available in a massive spectrum of colors and are perfect for retail storefronts in bustling areas like Sector 17 or Phase 7 Mohali.

### 2. Stainless Steel & Brass
For a strictly corporate or luxury aesthetic, solid metal is unmatched. We laser-cut SS (Stainless Steel) and brass, offering both brushed (matte) and mirror-polished finishes. These are highly favored by law firms, luxury real estate developers, and corporate HQs in Panchkula.

### 3. Back-Lit / Halo Effect Letters
A highly modern approach where the front of the letter is solid metal, and LEDs are mounted behind it. The light reflects off the mounting wall, creating a beautiful "halo" effect around the dark silhouette of the letter.

## Expert Installation

3D letters require precise, secure installation. Our local Tricity team uses heavy-duty commercial adhesives, stud mounting, and weatherproof wiring to ensure your sign remains perfectly aligned and brilliantly lit for years to come.
