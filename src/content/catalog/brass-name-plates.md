For a truly classic, authoritative, and vintage aesthetic, **Brass Name Plates** remain unparalleled. 

The heavy, golden hue of brass communicates heritage, permanence, and prestige. It is the traditional choice for established law firms, medical clinics, government offices, and heritage homes in Chandigarh.

## Deep Chemical Etching

We do not use cheap surface printing on our brass plates. At Classic Printing, we use a deep chemical etching process. 

Your name, designation, and address are etched deeply into the solid brass plate. These etched cavities are then filled with high-quality black or colored enamel paint and baked for permanence. This ensures the text will never rub off or fade.

## Maintenance and Patina

Brass is a living metal. Over time, if left untreated, it develops a beautiful, darkened natural patina (an antique look) due to oxidation. 

- **If you want the antique look:** We can leave the brass unsealed, allowing it to age naturally.
- **If you want the shiny gold look:** We coat the finished brass plate with a premium clear lacquer. This seals the metal from the air, preventing oxidation and keeping it looking shiny and new for years.
