When you need bright, evenly illuminated indoor advertising that allows you to change the graphics frequently, **Slim LED Clip-On Boards** are the perfect solution.

## What is a Clip-On Board?

A clip-on board is an ultra-slim aluminum frame (often less than an inch thick) that snaps open on all four sides. Inside, an edge-lit LED light guide panel (LGP) provides incredibly bright and uniform backlighting. 

You simply snap open the edges, insert a translucent backlit poster (printed on special backlit film), place the protective clear cover over it, and snap the edges shut. 

## Key Advantages

- **Rapid Graphic Changes:** You do not need a technician to change the advertisement. Any staff member can swap the poster in under 60 seconds.
- **Ultra-Slim Profile:** Unlike bulky outdoor glow signs, clip-on boards sit almost flush against the wall, making them highly elegant for tight indoor spaces.
- **Energy Efficient:** They use low-voltage edge-lit LEDs, consuming minimal electricity while providing stunning brightness.
