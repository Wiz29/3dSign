When you need temporary advertising, product labels, or fast shop-front branding, our **Sticker and Flex Board Printing** services deliver speed and quality.

## Custom Sticker Printing

We print and precisely die-cut custom stickers for any application. 

- **Product Labels:** High-quality, waterproof vinyl labels for bottles, packaging, and cosmetics.
- **Brand Decals:** Give-away stickers featuring your logo for laptops and bumpers.
- **Floor Graphics:** Anti-slip floor stickers to direct foot traffic in supermarkets or hospitals.

We print on premium SAV (Self Adhesive Vinyl) and offer gloss or matte lamination to ensure your stickers are scratch-resistant and weatherproof.

## Pre-Mounted Flex Boards

Sometimes, you don't just need a flex print; you need a ready-to-hang board. We provide complete flex boards where the printed flex is tightly stretched over a sturdy MS (Mild Steel) or wooden frame.

- **Shop Boards:** The classic, unlit shop front board. Highly cost-effective for local retailers in Kharar and Kurali.
- **To-Let & Sale Signs:** Rigid boards that can be tied to gates or poles for real estate agents.
- **Directional Signage:** Temporary event signage mounted on poles.
