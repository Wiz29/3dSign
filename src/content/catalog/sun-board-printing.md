When you need rigid, lightweight, and perfectly flat indoor signage, **Sun Board Printing** (also known as Foam Board) is the ideal choice. 

Sun boards are made of high-density PVC foam. They are incredibly light, easy to cut, and offer a smooth white surface that yields exceptionally vibrant print results.

## Applications of Sun Board Printing

Because it is rigid yet lightweight, Sun Board is the go-to material for a variety of indoor marketing and structural applications across the Chandigarh Tricity:

- **Exhibition & Event Stalls:** Wall paneling and branding for corporate events at the exhibition grounds.
- **Retail Point-of-Sale (POS):** Promotional cut-outs, dangling signs, and product displays inside supermarkets and showrooms.
- **Life-Size Cutouts & Standees:** Perfect for brand mascots, celebrity endorsements, or promotional stands with a back-support strut.
- **Office Information:** Safety signs, directional arrows, and temporary notices that look far more professional than laminated paper.

## Our Printing Technology

We offer two methods for Sun Board branding:
1. **Vinyl Mounting:** We print high-resolution graphics on premium self-adhesive vinyl and mount it flawlessly onto the Sun Board using cold-roll laminators to ensure zero bubbles or wrinkles.
2. **Direct UV Printing:** Using our advanced flatbed UV printers, we print the ink directly onto the surface of the Sun Board. The UV light instantly cures the ink, resulting in a scratch-resistant, matte finish that looks incredibly premium.
