When standard backlit flex isn't premium enough for your brand, **UV Fabric Backlit Printing** (also known as Tension Fabric) is the ultimate upgrade. 

If you walk through Elante Mall in Chandigarh or the Mohali airport, the massive, ultra-vibrant, perfectly smooth illuminated advertisements you see are not flex—they are UV printed fabrics.

## What is Tension Fabric Signage?

Instead of PVC flex, we use a specialized, stretchable, light-diffusing polyester fabric. We print your graphics directly onto this fabric using high-end UV printers. 

A silicone strip is sewn around the edges of the fabric. During installation, this strip is simply pushed into the edge of a sleek aluminum frame. The fabric pulls drum-tight, creating a perfectly flat, seamless graphic.

## Why Brands Choose UV Fabric

- **Zero Glare:** Unlike flex or acrylic which reflect ambient light and cause glare, fabric absorbs ambient light, ensuring your graphic looks deeply saturated and rich from any angle.
- **Seamless Large Formats:** Fabric can be printed in massive seamless rolls, allowing for gigantic light boxes without ugly welding joints in the middle of a face.
- **Eco-Friendly:** Fabric is PVC-free and significantly more environmentally friendly than traditional flex.
- **Easy Updates:** The fabric is incredibly lightweight and can be folded into a small box. We can ship replacement graphics to your retail store, and your own staff can swap out the fabric in minutes—no professional installers required.
