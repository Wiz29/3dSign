When it comes to covering large commercial facades or creating a perfectly flat, highly durable base for 3D letters, **Aluminum Composite Panels (ACP)** are the industry gold standard. 

## What is an ACP Sign Board?

An ACP board consists of a thermoplastic core sandwiched between two thin aluminum sheets. This creates a panel that is incredibly lightweight yet completely rigid and flat. 

At Classic Printing, we use premium ACP sheets as the foundation for our high-end signage projects across Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula.

## Why Choose ACP?

- **Zero Warping:** Unlike wood or thin plastics, ACP does not warp, bend, or expand significantly under the extreme heat of the Punjab summer.
- **Flawless Finish:** ACP provides a perfectly smooth, glossy, or matte metallic finish that looks highly professional.
- **Weatherproof:** It is 100% waterproof and rust-proof, ensuring your storefront looks brand new for decades.

## Applications

### 1. Base for 3D Letters
The most common use of ACP is as the backing board for illuminated 3D acrylic or steel letters. By routing the ACP, we can completely hide all the messy electrical wiring behind the panel, resulting in a clean, seamless look for your storefront.

### 2. Full Facade Cladding
If your building exterior looks old or uneven, we can clad the entire front facade in ACP panels before mounting your signage, instantly modernizing your business premises.
