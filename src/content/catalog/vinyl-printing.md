Transform empty walls, glass partitions, and company vehicles into powerful marketing assets with our high-resolution **Vinyl Printing** services. 

Self-adhesive vinyl (SAV) is an incredibly versatile printing medium. It consists of a printable PVC face, a sticky adhesive backing, and a release liner. Once printed, it can be applied to almost any smooth surface.

## Applications of Vinyl Printing

### 1. Glass & Storefront Branding
Turn your retail windows in Sector 17 or Phase 7 into massive billboards. We print vibrant graphics on opaque vinyl, clear vinyl, or frosted vinyl (for privacy with a premium etched-glass look).

### 2. Wall Wraps & Murals
Why paint your office walls when you can wrap them? We print custom wall graphics and corporate timelines that completely transform bland IT office interiors in Mohali into inspiring workspaces.

### 3. Vehicle Branding
Take your marketing on the road. From simple logo decals to full commercial vehicle wraps for your delivery fleet, we use premium cast vinyl that conforms to the curves of the vehicle and withstands outdoor weather.

## Lamination & Durability

To ensure your vinyl prints survive scratches, cleaning chemicals, and harsh sunlight, we apply a protective layer of cold lamination (available in Gloss or Matte finishes) over the print. This locks in the ink and dramatically extends the lifespan of the graphic.
